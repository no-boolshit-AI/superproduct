Question: How do we return to profitable growth after two flat years?

- Management proposes four moves: pricing & promotions (+€75m EBIT), assortment & private label (+€55m), store labour (+€50m), marketplace & procurement (+€80m). Capex €335m over three years.
- Decision requested: approve a transformation office, a €40m pilot budget and the 2027 wave plan.
