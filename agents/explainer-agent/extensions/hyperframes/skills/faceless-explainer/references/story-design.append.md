## explainer-agent: teach a newcomer

In explainer-agent runs, `hyperframes:clear-explainer` decides between the options above in favour of clarity — load it before writing `STORYBOARD.md`. In short:

- one driving question the viewer would ask; the answer (`message:`) in one sentence a 12-year-old understands;
- at most three main points (the spine), signposted; concrete before abstract (an everyday picture or analogy, then the rule); every term defined in plain words on first use, two new terms at most;
- one idea and one visual metaphor per frame, kept across frames (a consistent stage);
- ≤ 8 words per line on screen, one highlighted word per frame, rounded numbers with a comparison;
- a recap frame (the main points, one line each, with their pictures) before the call to action;
- the newcomer test on every frame before the storyboard is presented.
