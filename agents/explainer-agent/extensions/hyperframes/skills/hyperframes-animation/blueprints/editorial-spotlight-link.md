# editorial-spotlight-link — The Group → Spotlight on One → Connected to What It Explains

**intent**: The documentary move. Several pictures (people, places, things) share the frame; the light goes down on all but one; a ring draws around it; a slash name tag says who or what it is; a connector line with dots runs to the thing it is linked to, whose label lands last. The viewer learns a relationship, not a list. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Feature_showcase / mechanism: "this part does that" (the valve → the pressure; the clerk → head office).
- Story-explainer turn: the person who matters, singled out.
- Evidence: where a number or a decision comes from.

**duration**: 5–7 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~1.2s): [2–4 pictures] (B&W photo or dot-screen treatment, panels or cut-outs) are on the ground;
  the camera pushes slowly. Optionally one dimmed duplicate of the subject large in the back layer.
Scene 2 (~1.2–~2.4s): the spotlight closes on [the one]: everything else dims to ~30%; a ring draws around it.
Scene 3 (~2.2–~3.0s): [the name tag] "/ [Name]" rises beside it.
Scene 4 (~3.0–~4.4s): the first dot pops on [the one]; the connector routes (down, along, up) to [the other
  thing]; its dot pops; [its label] rises.
Scene 5 (~4.4s–end): held read.
```

**signature move**: the light goes down on everything but one subject — then a line ties it to something else.

**motion vocabulary**: spotlight (focus), ring draw, connector draw with dots, rise for tags, slow push.

**rule mapping**

- spotlight, ring, connector, name tag → `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- pictures and treatments → `rules/editorial-cutout.md`, `rules/editorial-kit.md`
- push → `rules/editorial-camera.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: the spotlit subject in the upper half; the connector runs through empty ground (never across faces or words) to the second label near an edge. **16:9**: the classic left name / right destination with the line along the lower third.

**stillness note**: hold ≥ 1.5 s after the last label; nothing else moves but the push.
