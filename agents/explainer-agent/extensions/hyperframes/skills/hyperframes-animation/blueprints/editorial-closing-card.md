# editorial-closing-card — Field Wipe → Closing Line → Logo and Call to Action

**intent**: The last frame: a field of the brand colour wipes across; the closing line (the thesis, distilled) rises in the display face; the logo file and the call to action land below; held to the end. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- CTA / Brand_Outro.

**duration**: 3.5–5 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~0.6s): [the field] wipes across from [the video's forward direction].
Scene 2 (~0.4–~1.6s): [the closing line] (≤ 8 words) rises; optionally [the hero cut-out] returns small
  (callback to the opener).
Scene 3 (~1.6–~2.6s): [the logo file] rises; [the CTA] lands as a pill ("[label] · [url]").
Scene 4 (~2.6s–end): held to the last frame; push only.
```

**signature move**: the colour field takes the whole frame — the brand's colour is the final image.

**motion vocabulary**: grow (field wipe), rise, pop (CTA), slow push.

**rule mapping**

- field wipe → `rules/editorial-transitions.md`
- the rest → `rules/editorial-kit.md`, `rules/editorial-cutout.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: line in the upper-middle; logo + CTA at y ≈ 60–75%; nothing in the bottom 17%. **16:9**: line left, logo + CTA right.

**stillness note**: hold ≥ 1.5 s after the CTA. The logo is the brand's file, never redrawn or treated.
