# collage-portrait-field — Torn Colour Field → Printed Subject Slapped On → The Hand Names It

**intent**: The collage's cinematic portrait. A big torn sheet of the accent colour (white torn rim) lands on the board; behind or beside it, a torn clipping filled with the key words (or one giant word / year); the subject — a generated picture printed in black-and-white halftone — slaps on top of the field; small drawn line-art makes the idea literal (strings from the top edge, an arrow, a loop around the face); the one line lands on a torn strip and gets the highlighter. Built on `collage-kit`.

**roles served**

- Hook: the person, object or situation the question is about.
- Product_Intro / concept: the idea embodied by one subject ("the puppet bureaucrat": strings drawn to the hands).
- Takeaway: the subject returns (callback) with the closing line.

**duration**: 5–6.5 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~0.8s): paper board, slow push starts. [The colour field] — a torn sheet in the accent
  (data-cg-fill accent, white rim), 55–75% of the frame — slaps in; [the giant word/year] or [a clipping of
  the key words] lands in the back layer, partly under it.
Scene 2 (~0.7–~1.6s): [the subject] (B&W halftone cut-out, data-color-grading on a clip <img>) slaps onto the
  field, upper 60% of the frame.
Scene 3 (~1.8–~3.2s): [the line-art] draws on twos when the idea is named: strings from the top edge to the
  hands, an arrow to [a small drawn prop], a loop around [the detail].
Scene 4 (~3.2–~4.2s): [the line] on a torn strip slaps in below; the highlighter swipes behind [the word].
Scene 5 (~4.2s–end): held read; boil; the push continues.
```

**signature move**: a black-and-white printed subject slapped onto a torn sheet of the brand colour — the colour is paper, the subject is print.

**motion vocabulary**: slap on twos (field, subject, strip), marker draw, highlighter, boil, push with three-layer parallax.

**rule mapping**

- the printed subject (treatment) → `rules/collage-halftone-figure.md`
- the field and strips → `rules/collage-paper-tear.md`
- slaps → `rules/collage-cutout-slap.md`
- line-art, highlighter → `rules/collage-marker-annotate.md`
- boil → `rules/collage-stop-motion-boil.md`
- push + parallax → `rules/collage-camera.md`
- scaffold → `rules/collage-kit.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: field + subject in the upper 60%, the subject ≥ 500 px wide (halftone needs size); the strip in the band y ≈ 66–80%; nothing in the bottom 17%. **16:9**: field + subject left 55%, strip right.

**stillness note**: hold ≥ 1.5 s after the highlighter (silent: 0.3 s per word on screen + 1 s).
