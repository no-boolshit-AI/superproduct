# editorial-hub — One Subject at the Centre → The Parts Around It, Connected

**intent**: A system explained as its parts: the subject in an arch niche with rays at the centre; around it, three to six panels (each a small picture or symbol in the same dot-screen treatment) appear one by one, each joined to the centre by a line. Monochrome brand colour on paper — the whole frame in one hue. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Feature_showcase (concept-explainer): "it's made of these parts".
- Product_intro: what a thing connects (identity → licence, diploma, fingerprint, voice).
- Takeaway: everything the video showed, gathered around its subject.

**duration**: 5.5–7.5 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~1.2s): paper ground in the brand's light tint; [the arch] (ed-field arch, brand colour) grows up
  from the bottom at the centre; rays pop around it; [the subject] rises inside (dot-screen in the brand hue).
Scene 2 (~1.2–~4.2s): [panel 1 … N] (rounded outlines, each with one small picture/symbol + a mono label)
  rise one by one, in reading order, each joined to the arch by a line that draws as it lands.
Scene 3 (~4.2s–end): held read; the push pulls back slightly (push −0.03) to show the whole system.
```

**signature move**: a single colour for everything and lines from a centre — the parts are visibly ONE system.

**motion vocabulary**: grow (arch), pop (rays), rise (panels), line draw, a gentle pull-back.

**rule mapping**

- arch, subject, dot-screen treatment → `rules/editorial-cutout.md`, `rules/editorial-kit.md`
- lines, rays → `rules/editorial-graphics.md`, `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- pull-back → `rules/editorial-camera.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: arch centred at y ≈ 45%, 40% of the width; panels in two columns above and below; labels ≥ 28 px. **16:9**: arch centred, panels in a ring left and right.

**stillness note**: with 5+ panels, the hold is ≥ 2 s — the viewer needs to scan.
