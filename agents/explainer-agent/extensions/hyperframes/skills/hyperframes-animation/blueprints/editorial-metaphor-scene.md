# editorial-metaphor-scene — A Subject in a Situation That Explains It

**intent**: The mechanism shown as a small staged scene: the subject cut-out at work with one or two props (a typewriter, a desk plane, a stack of paper) and line-art that makes the metaphor literal (puppet strings from the top of the frame, arrows of flow, a leash, a scale). One image that a newcomer understands without words. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Feature_showcase / mechanism: how it works, as one picture ("the clerk is a puppet of the system").
- Concept: the analogy that makes the idea concrete.
- Problem: the situation that causes the pain.

**duration**: 5–7 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~1.0s): field ground; [the set] — a flat grey plane (a desk, a floor, a shelf: a slanted
  ed-field) — grows in; [prop 1] rises onto it.
Scene 2 (~0.8–~2.0s): [the subject] (B&W halftone) rises into the set, interacting with the prop.
Scene 3 (~2.0–~3.4s): [the metaphor line-art] draws on — strings from the top edge to the hands, arrows
  of flow, a leash — exactly when the voiceover (or the line on screen) names the metaphor.
Scene 4 (~3.4–~4.6s): [the line] (≤ 8 words) rises in the empty field; one word highlighted.
Scene 5 (~4.6s–end): held read; a small repeated motion is allowed (a sheet of paper lifts and settles once).
```

**signature move**: the metaphor is DRAWN onto a printed scene — line-art makes the analogy literal.

**motion vocabulary**: grow (set), rise (props, subject), line draw (the metaphor), highlighter, push.

**rule mapping**

- set, props, subject → `rules/editorial-cutout.md`
- metaphor lines → `rules/editorial-graphics.md` (line, route, wave) and `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- push → `rules/editorial-camera.md`
- scaffold → `rules/editorial-kit.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: the scene in the upper 60% (the set's plane across the frame at y ≈ 55–65%); the line below. **16:9**: scene centre-right, the line on the left third.

**stillness note**: the scene is a picture to look at — hold ≥ 1.5 s after the line.
