# collage-stamp-title-card — Tear Open → Ink Band Card → CTA Sticker → Stamp

**intent**: The collage close. A covering sheet tears away; an ink band (torn, white rim) slaps in carrying the closing line; the call to action lands as an accent sticker/pill; the logo file is placed as a cut-out; the URL is taped on; a rubber stamp thumps the launch word; the card holds to the last frame. Built on `collage-kit`.

**roles served**

- CTA: closing line + action + URL + logo.
- Brand_Outro: wordmark + signature line.
- Launch announcement: the stamp carries the news word from the brief ("Now live", "Out now", "Launch").

**duration**: 4–6 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~0.8s): the frame opens on a closed cover sheet (the previous frame cuts to it);
  ~0.25 s in it tears in two and both halves fly off, revealing the paper ground.
Scene 2 (~0.7–~1.6s): [the ink band] — torn all round with a white rim, the contrast band of
  the video — slaps in, centred upper half, carrying [the closing line] (serif or display, paper
  colour).
Scene 3 (~1.6–~2.6s): [the CTA] — an accent pill/sticker with ink text, e.g. "[action]" — slaps
  onto the band's lower edge; [the logo file] lands as a small cut-out; [the URL] lands on a
  paper strip and a tape pins it.
Scene 4 (~2.6–~3.1s): [the stamp] — 1–3 uppercase words from the brief — thumps onto the paper
  beside the band.
Scene 5 (~3.1s–end): held, everything boiling; the camera push continues; the final frame
  is the readable card.
```

**signature move**: the stamp thump on a finished card — the last physical act, then stillness.

**motion vocabulary**: tear-away reveal, band slap, sticker slap, logo cut-out slap, URL strip + tape, stamp, boil, push.

**rule mapping**

- tear-away cover, torn band with rim → `rules/collage-paper-tear.md`
- band / sticker / logo / URL arrivals → `rules/collage-cutout-slap.md`
- tape, stamp → `rules/collage-marker-annotate.md`
- boil → `rules/collage-stop-motion-boil.md`
- push → `rules/collage-camera.md`
- scaffold → `rules/collage-kit.md`

**layout (4:5)**: band ~88% wide in y ≈ 18–60%; CTA pill overlapping the band's bottom edge; logo and URL strip below the band; stamp to one side at an angle (−6…−12°); bottom 17% clear.

**content rule**: the logo is the supplied file (never redrawn); CTA label and URL verbatim from the product; the stamp word is from the brief.
