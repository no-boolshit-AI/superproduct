# editorial-crowd — Rows of the Same Figure → The Odd One Out

**intent**: Scale, conformity, "everyone does this": the same cut-out figure repeated in rows at two or three depths, parallax pulling the rows apart; then the one figure that is different lands — or the line that names the pattern. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Problem: "millions of people do X" made visible.
- Concept: conformity, a system, a market — many identical units.
- Turn: the exception (the one in colour, the one facing the other way).

**duration**: 4.5–6 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~1.4s): ground split by a field band; [back row] of the figure (smaller, two-ink or dark
  treatment) rises with a short stagger; [front row] (larger, B&W halftone) rises after it.
Scene 2 (~1.4–~2.6s): the push and the depth parallax make the rows slide past each other.
Scene 3 (~2.6–~3.6s): [the odd one out] lands — in colour, or turned, or the spotlight finds it — OR
  [the line naming the pattern] rises across the field band.
Scene 4 (~3.6s–end): held read.
```

**signature move**: one figure, many times — rows at different depths — and then the exception.

**motion vocabulary**: staggered rise, parallax between rows, pop or spotlight for the exception.

**rule mapping**

- repeated figures, treatments → `rules/editorial-cutout.md`
- the exception's spotlight → `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- rows' parallax → `rules/editorial-camera.md`
- scaffold → `rules/editorial-kit.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: 2–3 rows across the full width, figures cropped at the frame edges (the crowd goes on); the field band behind the middle row. **16:9**: longer rows, the exception right of centre.

**stillness note**: the rows keep drifting (push), but no new figure arrives after the exception.
