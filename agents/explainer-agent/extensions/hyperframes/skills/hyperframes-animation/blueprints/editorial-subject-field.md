# editorial-subject-field — Colour Field → Printed Subject → The Line That Names It

**intent**: The editorial opener and the "meet the idea" frame. A flat field of brand colour wipes in; behind it, a page of the key words (a text-filled silhouette) or one giant word; the subject — a black-and-white halftone cut-out — rises into place in front; the one line lands under it and its key word gets the highlighter. The camera has been pushing in, with parallax, the whole time. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Hook: the person, object or situation the question is about ("Why do ordinary people follow orders?" over a clerk printed in halftone on red).
- Product_Intro / concept: the named idea, embodied by one subject ("This is the bystander effect.").
- Takeaway: the subject returns (callback) with the generalised line.

**duration**: 4.5–6.5 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~0.8s): [field] (shape: arch / blob / full ground, the brand colour) wipes in from [edge];
  the camera push starts. Behind it, [the text-filled silhouette of the key term] or [one giant word] rises.
Scene 2 (~0.5–~1.6s): [the subject] — a B&W halftone cut-out with a white rim — rises into the field's
  centre-of-interest (upper 60% of the frame). 3 depth layers drift at different speeds.
Scene 3 (~1.8–~3.2s): [the line] (display face, 1–2 lines, ≤ 8 words) rises under the subject when it is
  read; the highlighter sweeps behind [the one key word].
Scene 4 (~3.2s–end): held read. Only the push and the grain move.
```

**signature move**: the subject is printed in black and white and the COLOUR belongs to the field behind it — with type or a page of words layered between them.

**motion vocabulary**: field wipe (grow), rise (subject, type), highlighter, slow push with parallax, live grain.

**rule mapping**

- field, subject, text-fill, giant word → `rules/editorial-cutout.md`
- push + parallax → `rules/editorial-camera.md`
- highlighter → `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- scaffold, treatments, helpers → `rules/editorial-kit.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: field + subject in the upper 60%, the subject 45–60% of the frame width; the line in the band y ≈ 66–80%; nothing in the bottom 17%. **16:9**: field + subject on the left 55%, the line on the right.

**stillness note**: Scene 4 holds ≥ 1.2 s (silent video: ≥ 0.3 s per word on screen). No extra pieces after the highlighter.
