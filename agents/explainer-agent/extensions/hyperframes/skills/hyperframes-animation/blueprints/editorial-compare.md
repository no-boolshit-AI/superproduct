# editorial-compare — Two Things Side by Side → The Difference Is the Point

**intent**: A newcomer understands a number by comparison. Two stacks, two bars, two cut-outs or two numbers grow side by side; one is in the field colour (the point), the other in ink; the gap between them is labelled. Built on `editorial-kit`.

**roles served**

- Social_proof / evidence: before vs after, us vs them, now vs then.
- Benefit_highlight: how much more / less.
- Problem: the gap that hurts.

**duration**: 4.5–6 s.

**shot structure**

```
Scene 1 (0.0–~0.8s): paper ground with a faint grid; a baseline rule draws across.
Scene 2 (~0.6–~2.2s): [thing A] (ink: coin stack / bar / cut-out) grows from the baseline with [its number]
  counting up and [its label] below; then [thing B] (the field colour) grows taller/shorter beside it.
Scene 3 (~2.2–~3.2s): [the gap] is named: a thin rule with arrow ticks spans the difference, and its label
  ("5× more", "half the time") rises — the highlighter on the multiplier.
Scene 4 (~3.2s–end): held read.
```

**signature move**: the comparison is spatial — two things grown from one baseline, the difference labelled.

**motion vocabulary**: line draw, grow from bottom, count-up, rise, highlighter.

**rule mapping**

- stacks, bars, count-ups, rules → `rules/editorial-graphics.md`
- highlighter → `rules/editorial-callouts.md`
- scaffold → `rules/editorial-kit.md`

**layout (4:5 / 9:16)**: the two things side by side in the middle 60% of the width, baseline at y ≈ 72%; numbers above them; the gap label to the right. **16:9**: same, with more air.

**stillness note**: numbers need two reads: hold ≥ 1.5 s.
