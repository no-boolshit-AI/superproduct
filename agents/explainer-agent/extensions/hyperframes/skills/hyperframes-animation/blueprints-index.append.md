## Collage blueprints (vox-collage)

Shapes for the Vox-style paper-collage look (the `vox-collage` skill and frame preset). Each is built on `collage-kit` and the `collage-*` rules; pick them when frame.md is the `vox-collage` preset. They follow the same Reproduce / Adapt / Compose method.

<blueprints>
<blueprint id="collage-poster-build" roles="Hook, Problem, Product_Intro" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A paper board fills with small cut-outs carrying the evidence (tags, labels, scraps) that pile up on twos; the torn headline strip slaps **on top of the pile** when it is read; one highlighter swipe marks the verdict word; tape pins it; slow push-in. The collage opener.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-clipping-highlight" roles="Problem, Key_Feature, Benefits, Takeaway" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A torn clipping lands with the claim; a hand annotates it like an editor — strikes the wrong phrase, circles or highlights the right one, draws an arrow to a counter-note that slaps on with the correction. The annotation is the argument.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-board-pan" roles="Benefits, Key_Feature, Product_Intro" duration="5–8s">
N offers / steps / features as tickets, cards or tags on a paper board bigger than the frame; the camera travels station to station, each piece slapping in as the camera arrives; a final pull-back shows the whole set.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-figure-plate" roles="Hook, Problem, Product_Intro" duration="4.5–6.5s">
"FIG. NN" — an invented ink-and-halftone drawing of the script's metaphor on a paper plate; parts move on twos; paper tags and marker arrows label the parts in sequence; the caption line lands and its key word is highlighted or struck and replaced.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-checklist" roles="Benefits, Takeaway, Product_Intro" duration="4–6s">
A torn notepad sheet; each line lands as a paper strip in reading order and a marker tick draws in its box; the one line to remember gets the highlighter. Deliverables, steps, guarantees — verbatim from the brief.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-stamp-title-card" roles="CTA, Brand_Outro" duration="4–6s">
A cover sheet tears away; an ink band (torn, white rim) slaps in with the closing line; the CTA lands as an accent sticker; the logo file as a cut-out; the URL taped on; a rubber stamp thumps the launch word; held to the last frame.
</blueprint>
</blueprints>

**Collage role menu** — Hook: `collage-poster-build`, `collage-figure-plate` · Problem: `collage-clipping-highlight`, `collage-poster-build` · Product_Intro: `collage-figure-plate`, `collage-board-pan` · Key_Feature / Benefits: `collage-board-pan`, `collage-clipping-highlight`, `collage-checklist` · Takeaway: `collage-checklist`, `collage-clipping-highlight` · CTA / Brand_Outro: `collage-stamp-title-card`. Any core blueprint can still be used in a collage video when its signature move is rebuilt with the kit (e.g. `kinetic-type-beats` as ransom-chip beats).
