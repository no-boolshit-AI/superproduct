## Collage blueprints (vox-collage)

Shapes for the Vox-style paper-collage look (the `vox-collage` skill and frame preset). Each is built on `collage-kit` and the `collage-*` rules; pick them when frame.md is the `vox-collage` preset. They follow the same Reproduce / Adapt / Compose method.

<blueprints>
<blueprint id="collage-poster-build" roles="Hook, Problem, Product_Intro" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A paper board fills with small cut-outs carrying the evidence (tags, labels, scraps) that pile up on twos; the torn headline strip slaps **on top of the pile** when it is read; one highlighter swipe marks the verdict word; tape pins it; slow push-in. The collage opener.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-clipping-highlight" roles="Problem, Key_Feature, Benefits, Takeaway" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A torn clipping lands with the claim; a hand annotates it like an editor — strikes the wrong phrase, circles or highlights the right one, draws an arrow to a counter-note that slaps on with the correction. The annotation is the argument.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-board-pan" roles="Benefits, Key_Feature, Product_Intro" duration="5–8s">
N offers / steps / features as tickets, cards or tags on a paper board bigger than the frame; the camera travels station to station, each piece slapping in as the camera arrives; a final pull-back shows the whole set.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-figure-plate" roles="Hook, Problem, Product_Intro" duration="4.5–6.5s">
"FIG. NN" — an invented ink-and-halftone drawing of the script's metaphor on a paper plate; parts move on twos; paper tags and marker arrows label the parts in sequence; the caption line lands and its key word is highlighted or struck and replaced.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-checklist" roles="Benefits, Takeaway, Product_Intro" duration="4–6s">
A torn notepad sheet; each line lands as a paper strip in reading order and a marker tick draws in its box; the one line to remember gets the highlighter. Deliverables, steps, guarantees — verbatim from the brief.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-portrait-field" roles="Hook, Product_Intro, Takeaway" duration="5–6.5s">
A torn sheet of the accent colour (white rim) lands; a clipping of the key words or a giant year behind it; the subject — a generated picture printed in B&W halftone — slaps on top; drawn line-art makes the idea literal (strings, an arrow, a loop); the line lands on a torn strip with one highlighter swipe. The cinematic collage portrait.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="collage-stamp-title-card" roles="CTA, Brand_Outro" duration="4–6s">
A cover sheet tears away; an ink band (torn, white rim) slaps in with the closing line; the CTA lands as an accent sticker; the logo file as a cut-out; the URL taped on; a rubber stamp thumps the launch word; held to the last frame.
</blueprint>
</blueprints>

**Collage role menu** — Hook: `collage-poster-build`, `collage-portrait-field`, `collage-figure-plate` · Problem: `collage-clipping-highlight`, `collage-poster-build` · Product_Intro: `collage-portrait-field`, `collage-figure-plate`, `collage-board-pan` · Key_Feature / Benefits: `collage-board-pan`, `collage-clipping-highlight`, `collage-checklist` · Takeaway (the recap): `collage-checklist`, `collage-clipping-highlight`, `collage-portrait-field` (callback) · CTA / Brand_Outro: `collage-stamp-title-card`. Any core blueprint can still be used in a collage video when its signature move is rebuilt with the kit (e.g. `kinetic-type-beats` as ransom-chip beats).

## Editorial blueprints (editorial)

Shapes for the cinematic editorial look (the `editorial` skill and frame preset): printed cut-outs on brand-colour fields, line-art callouts, a camera with parallax. Each is built on `editorial-kit` and the `editorial-*` rules; pick them when frame.md is the `editorial` preset. Same Reproduce / Adapt / Compose method.

<blueprints>
<blueprint id="editorial-subject-field" roles="Hook, Product_Intro, Takeaway" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A brand-colour field wipes in; a page of the key words (text-filled silhouette) or one giant word behind; the subject — a B&W halftone cut-out with a white rim — rises in front; the one line lands below with one highlighted word. The editorial opener.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-type-behind" roles="Social_Proof, Product_Intro, Problem" duration="4–6s">
A year, a number or one word set enormous in the field colour; a B&W cut-out stands in front, overlapping it; a small label and a thin rule say what it means. Parallax separates type and subject.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-spotlight-link" roles="Key_Feature, Social_Proof, Problem" duration="5–7s">
Several pictures share the frame; the light goes down on all but one; a ring draws around it; a "/ Name" tag rises; a connector with dots routes to what it is linked to, whose label lands last. The documentary relationship shot.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-crowd" roles="Problem, Product_Intro, Benefits" duration="4.5–6s">
The same cut-out figure in rows at two or three depths, parallax pulling the rows apart; then the odd one out lands (in colour, turned, or spotlit) — or the line naming the pattern. Scale and conformity.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-metaphor-scene" roles="Key_Feature, Product_Intro, Problem" duration="5–7s">
The mechanism as a small staged scene: the subject at work with one or two props on a flat set; line-art makes the metaphor literal (puppet strings, flow arrows, a leash) exactly when it is named; the line lands with one highlighted word.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-orbit" roles="Product_Intro, Benefits, Hook" duration="4.5–6.5s">
A small cut-out at the centre; thin rings or tilted orbits draw outward around it; numbered markers pop on as each ring is named; a two-colour headline above. Scale made visible.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-hub" roles="Key_Feature, Product_Intro, Takeaway" duration="5.5–7.5s">
The subject in an arch niche with rays at the centre, in one brand hue on paper; three to six panels around it land one by one, each joined to the centre by a line. A system as its parts.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-compare" roles="Social_Proof, Benefits, Problem" duration="4.5–6s">
Two stacks, bars, cut-outs or numbers grow from one baseline — one in ink, one in the field colour — with their numbers counting up; the gap between them is spanned and labelled ("5× more"). The comparison is the explanation.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-chart-highlight" roles="Social_Proof, Benefits" duration="5–7s">
A quiet sourced chart: title, mono source line, a few bars growing on a drafting grid; then the highlighter (or the field colour) lights up the one category the explanation is about.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-recap" roles="Takeaway" duration="4.5–6.5s">
The main points return as short numbered lines, each with the small cut-out that taught it (a callback), landing one by one; the one to remember gets the highlighter. The TED-Ed recap before the call to action.
</blueprint>

<blueprint id="editorial-closing-card" roles="CTA, Brand_Outro" duration="3.5–5s">
The brand-colour field wipes across the frame; the closing line rises; the logo file and the CTA pill land; held to the end.
</blueprint>
</blueprints>
