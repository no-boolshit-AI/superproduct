## Paper collage (vox-collage)

Vox-style cut-paper collage. Every recipe here is built on `collage-kit` (the shared `assets/collage/collage.js` kit) — cite `collage-kit` in every collage frame's `rules:` so its packet carries the scaffold. Style guide and storyboard method: the `vox-collage` skill.

<rules>
<collage-kit path="rules/collage-kit.md">The paper-collage kit every vox-collage frame is built on — scaffold (ground, tone, board, layers, grain), CSS variables, `.cg-*` classes, `data-cg-*` attributes and the `Collage.*` timeline helpers (slap, slide, lift, ransom, tape, stamp, highlight, draw, boil, drift, camera, tearAway). Seek-safe, deterministic, no clip-path/blur hazards. Tags: collage, vox, paper, kit, scaffold, texture</collage-kit>
<collage-cutout-slap path="rules/collage-cutout-slap.md">Cut-out paper pieces land on twos — arrive lifted (bigger, turned, long shadow via `--lift`) and settle with a small overshoot; slide in from off the board, ransom-chip headlines, hard cut-ins, lift-off exits. Timing table for hero vs small pieces and group staggers. Tags: collage, cut-out, slap, entrance, exit, ransom, stop-motion</collage-cutout-slap>
<collage-paper-tear path="rules/collage-paper-tear.md">Torn strips and sheets drawn as SVG from authored sizes (torn edges, seeds, white rim under coloured paper) and the tear-away reveal: a covering sheet rips along a ragged seam and both halves fly off on twos. Tags: collage, paper, torn, rim, tear-away, reveal</collage-paper-tear>
<collage-stop-motion-boil path="rules/collage-stop-motion-boil.md">The collage cadence — pieces on twos (12 poses/s, render at 24 fps), camera smooth; held pieces boil (±1 px, ±0.4°) from a hash of quantized time. Tags: collage, stop-motion, twos, boil, jitter, 24fps</collage-stop-motion-boil>
<collage-camera path="rules/collage-camera.md">Rostrum camera over a paper board — slow smooth push-in with layer parallax for one station; smooth moves between stations on a board bigger than the frame, pieces landing on arrival, final pull-back. Tags: collage, camera, push, parallax, pan, stations</collage-camera>
<collage-marker-annotate path="rules/collage-marker-annotate.md">Editorial annotation — highlighter behind one word, marker loops, arrows, underlines, strike-throughs, scribbles, ticks and crosses drawn on (unscaled SVG, pathLength-normalised), tape strips and rubber stamps. Tags: collage, marker, highlighter, circle, arrow, strike, tick, tape, stamp</collage-marker-annotate>
<collage-halftone-figure path="rules/collage-halftone-figure.md">Invented ink-and-halftone illustrations on a paper plate with a FIG. caption (SVG outlines + dot-pattern shading, parts moving on twos); halftone/duotone/print treatment and cut-outs for real photos only when supplied and cleared. Tags: collage, halftone, illustration, figure, duotone, print</collage-halftone-figure>
</rules>

## Editorial cut-out (editorial)

Cinematic editorial explainer look — black-and-white halftone cut-outs on flat brand-colour fields, type behind the subject, line-art callouts, a camera with parallax, film grain. Every recipe here is built on `editorial-kit` (the shared `assets/editorial/editorial.js` kit) — cite `editorial-kit` in every editorial frame's `rules:` so its packet carries the scaffold. Style guide and storyboard method: the `editorial` skill.

<rules>
<editorial-kit path="rules/editorial-kit.md">The editorial kit every editorial frame is built on — scaffold (ground, camera, depth layers, spotlight, film finish), CSS variables, `.ed-*` classes, `data-ed-*` attributes, the picture treatments (HyperFrames `data-color-grading` recipes: B&W halftone, brand dot screen, two-ink, B&W photo, print colour) and the `Editorial.*` helpers (push, camera, rise, pop, grow, out, cut, draw, highlight, focus, connect, count, grain). Seek-safe, deterministic, no clip-path/blur/radial-gradient hazards. Tags: editorial, kit, scaffold, cinematic, halftone, grain, vignette</editorial-kit>
<editorial-camera path="rules/editorial-camera.md">The cinematic camera — a slow push with 2.5D parallax between depth layers on every frame, reframing on a detail, stations, and the zoom-through that hands one frame to the next. Tags: editorial, camera, push, parallax, depth, reframe, zoom-through</editorial-camera>
<editorial-cutout path="rules/editorial-cutout.md">Subjects as printed cut-outs on flat colour — B&W halftone (or brand dot-screen) pictures over colour fields shaped as arch/circle/blob/slant, giant type behind the subject, the text-filled silhouette, rows of repeated figures, and their entrances. Tags: editorial, cut-out, halftone, duotone, colour-field, type-behind, silhouette, crowd</editorial-cutout>
<editorial-callouts path="rules/editorial-callouts.md">Pointing the eye — the spotlight that dims all but the subject, a ring drawn around it, documentary connector lines with dots and slash name tags, the highlighter behind the key word. Tags: editorial, spotlight, focus, ring, connector, name-tag, highlight, documentary</editorial-callouts>
<editorial-graphics path="rules/editorial-graphics.md">Line-art and data — concentric rings and orbits with markers, rays, coin stacks, waves, the big number that counts up with its comparison, simple sourced bar charts that grow. Tags: editorial, line-art, rings, orbit, rays, stack, chart, bars, count-up, data</editorial-graphics>
<editorial-transitions path="rules/editorial-transitions.md">Edited seams — colour-field wipes that open a frame, the iris, match cuts on shape and colour, the zoom-through; which registry transition to use between frames (mostly cut). Tags: editorial, transition, wipe, iris, match-cut, zoom-through, opener</editorial-transitions>
</rules>
