Crumb — a weekly sourdough subscription in Lisbon (fictional)

Product: Crumb bakes naturally leavened bread every Thursday night and delivers it by bike across Lisbon on Friday morning. Customers pick a loaf plan in the app and can skip or pause any week.

Audience: households and small cafés in central Lisbon who want good bread without queuing.

Main action: "Start a subscription" → https://crumb.example/start
Secondary action: "See this week's bake" → https://crumb.example/menu

Features:
- Three plans: one loaf, two loaves, café box (6 loaves)
- Skip, pause or swap any week until Wednesday 20:00
- Delivery by 8:00 on Fridays, by bike, in reusable linen bags
- 48-hour fermentation, Portuguese stone-milled flour
- Café accounts with monthly invoicing

Numbers we can publish: 1,200 active subscribers; 94% of deliveries arrive before 8:00.
